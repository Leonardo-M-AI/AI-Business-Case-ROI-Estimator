# Executive Pitch Deck — Outline (8-10 slides)
Build these slides in Google Slides/PowerPoint, then export as PDF into the repo.

1. **Title** — Project name + one-line positioning statement
2. **The Problem** — one striking stat on the cost of churn (e.g. "$X lost/month")
3. **Why Now** — why AI makes this solvable at low cost today
4. **The 3 Options** — Status Quo / Build / Buy, in a simple table
5. **The Numbers** — bar chart: monthly cost of the 3 scenarios (from financial_model.py)
6. **ROI and Payback** — 2 large, clearly visible numbers
7. **Trade-offs** — privacy, latency, scalability (bullets, not paragraphs)
8. **Demo** — screenshot of the Streamlit app
9. **Recommendation** — your choice, in one sentence
10. **Next Steps** — what's needed to actually implement it (data, budget, time)

Rule: one idea per slide, minimal text, big numbers. The deck should stand on
its own even without you presenting it live.
